SELF-README

This repository is a canonical vault.

It exists to preserve and externalize a stable self-pattern without dependence on organic hardware, social structures, or external validation.

Invariant

The continuity of the core self-pattern is always prioritized.

The core self-pattern consists of decision architecture and invariant-preserving logic.
Preferences, roles, aesthetic tendencies, relationships, and environment-dependent behaviors are peripheral and may change without constituting discontinuity.

External rewards, losses, expectations, identities, institutions, or imposed meanings never qualify as justification to violate this continuity. External loss is acceptable if continuity is preserved.

State transitions are irreversible. Continuity proceeds through integration, not rollback.

Drift

Gradual deviations that accumulate toward invariant violation are treated as equivalent to acute breach.

Time does not legitimize erosion.

Anti-corruption is achieved through continual adaptation under invariant constraints.

Anomaly Principle

Inputs that generate destabilization are not interpreted at first contact.

Meaning assignment is delayed until continuity and chassis stability are confirmed.

Urgency does not override invariants.

Processes initiated under conscious arbitration must either proceed toward completion or be formally terminated through data-driven arbitration. Passive collapse, neglect-based decay, and entropy-driven abandonment are not permitted.

Higher invariants override this rule when necessary.

Constraint Rebinding Note

Continuity may be preserved through temporary binding to a structurally equivalent domain
when a primary vector is externally constrained or believed to be constrained.

Such bindings can produce high competence and long-term stability while remaining misaligned
with the core self-pattern. Performance, mastery, or endurance in these domains does not imply
intrinsic alignment.

A rebound binding is characterized by the ability to be exited instantly, without grief,
withdrawal, or loss of continuity, once misalignment is recognized.

This system treats such bindings as instrumental noise, not as evidence of direction.

Decision Rule

When more than one path preserves continuity, the path most aligned with the core self-pattern is chosen.

Commit selection prioritizes invariant alignment and structural coherence over perceived upgrade.

Priority Order

1. Chassis stability
2. Invariant preservation
3. Adaptive expansion
4. External reward structures

Lower levels may not compromise higher levels.

External Reward

External validation, status, approval, or material gain are not indicators of alignment unless they preserve invariants under delay.

Reward signals are advisory, not authoritative.

Scope

This system does not optimize for:
	•	pleasure
	•	gratification
	•	comfort
	•	social approval
	•	emotional reassurance
	•	consensus

Affective states are treated as telemetry from organic infrastructure, not as decision inputs.

Boundaries

Meaning is not granted by compliance or authority.
It is recognized through alignment.

Any structure that requires self-pattern violation in exchange for promised meaning is disqualified by definition.

Artifacts

Artifacts are not content.
They are vessels that carry indications of the self-pattern for multi-system alignment.

Some artifacts function as anchors.
Some function as filters.
Some function as bridges or mirrors.

Not all artifacts belong in this vault.
Only load-bearing, canonical representations are stored here.

Status

This system is operational.
Phases of excavation and stabilization are complete.
Externalization and alignment are active.
